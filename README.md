# Open Board (IVC District 321)

Search two years of Illinois Valley Central school board minutes and agendas, browse every vote, and prepare a public comment or a FOIA request. Unofficial student prototype, not affiliated with the district.

- `index.html` is the whole app (no build step, no account, runs in the browser).
- `data.json` holds 40 meetings (Jul 2024 to Jun 2026), 4,832 text passages and 151 votes extracted from the district's published agenda and minutes Google Docs.
- Sources: https://www.ivcschools.com/school-board/board-agenda-minutes , the 2026-27 public notice of board meetings, the public comment guide and the FOIA page on ivcschools.com.
- Vote tallies and topic labels are extracted by software; always check the linked official minutes.
